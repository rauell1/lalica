# Content provenance

Every claim published on the Lalica Engineering Limited website traces back
to the company supplied profile document, `Lalica Company Profile_.pdf`
(the source PDF), which is kept unmodified at the repository root. Physical
page numbers below refer to the pages of that PDF, counting the cover as
page 1. The PDF's own printed footer numbers run one behind the physical
pages (printed "01" appears on physical page 2).

The source PDF is treated as company supplied evidence. It is not treated
as independent confirmation of registrations, partner relationships,
performance history, or legal status. Anything that the PDF does not state
is not published.

## Editorial notes

- The source PDF repeats the Mechanical Engineering item text on physical
  page 8 under the Refrigeration and HVAC heading. The site publishes each
  item once, under its correct service group.
- The PDF names four service areas in its introduction but describes three
  numbered groups. The site follows the three numbered groups exactly.
- Body copy on the site is lightly edited from the PDF for grammar and
  consistent sentence case. Facts (names, numbers, categories) are
  unchanged. All edits keep the original meaning.
- No establishment date, registration number, staff count, coverage claim,
  project history, client name, testimonial, certification, award, CSR
  figure, business hours, map coordinate, WhatsApp number, or social link
  is published, because none is stated in the source PDF.
